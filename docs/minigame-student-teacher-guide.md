# Mini-game edition: student and educator guide

## Students

Choose any of four characters; appearances have no advantage. Start in Pelvis Academy. Use arrows/WASD or the on-screen direction pad to approach a patient, then enter the case. Accessible shortcuts are available. After the academy, choose the three patient cases in any order. The final Trauma Shift unlocks after all four case rounds are reviewed.

Sort, match, select, arrange, locate and adjust the illustrated activities. Every drag has a select-card/choose-bin alternative. Review the evidence after each response. If a response needs correction, repeat the activity and explicitly review its explanation. Explain your reason before SBAR construction; chart/source links remain available. Confidence is optional and not rewarded.

References summarize your supplied course resources; original PDFs open at their source pages. Annotated patient teaching figures appear in feedback. An illustration is not a diagnostic image, measured pressure or procedural instruction. Work with the senior team in every patient story.

Practice rewards are 10 first-correct or 8 corrected, once. Retrieval stars are cosmetic. Both routes unlock the best clothing level when everything is completed. There is no default timer, random reward, streak or speed-based ranking. Optional sound starts off. Optional rush practice shows elapsed time only and does not change learning performance.

Review handovers and your learning evidence, then choose **Finish shift**. In demo mode results remain on your device; there is no course receipt or real classmate ranking. In connected mode synchronize and check confirmation before class. Pending/rejected work remains saved locally. Initial download needs internet; device storage can be cleared/evicted. Do not sign out before syncing, and never enter real patient data.

## Educators

Use **class priorities plus individual review**. Choose the content version before interpreting results; do not compare old 15-question attempts with this 44-station edition. Missing responses are missing evidence, not wrong answers. Objective tables give counts/denominators; exports provide percentages among observed responses, not the whole enrolled class.

Start with participation/submission, unresolved corrections, handovers awaiting review and the misconception map. Open the learner's actual raw game answers, correction history, reference use, optional confidence and handover reason. A completed memory board may contain wrong earlier pairs: inspect trials/score, not just PASS. Rewards are encouragement, not an assessment grade.

Download cohort, objective, detailed evidence, misconception and teacher-review CSVs separately. These UTF-8 Excel-compatible files also import into Google Sheets through **File → Import → Upload**. Keep identifiable comments in institution-approved storage; the printable class briefing intentionally excludes individual comments. Automatic transfer to a shared spreadsheet is not configured by this release.

### Suggested in-class recall and comparison

Ask learners to reconstruct the threat/priority/uncertainty before opening their pre-class note, then compare it with their saved reasoning:

- Red Alert: why does a transient response require further urgent planning? Which sources besides pelvis remain possible?
- Hidden Fracture: why is a normal current BP different from mechanical stability? What can an AP under a binder hide?
- Danger Down Below: what does meatal blood establish, and what remains unproven? How do suspected wound communication and closed degloving differ?
- Compare inlet/outlet purposes and source-angle discrepancies; compare H19/S29 antibiotic tables without turning adult source doses into a child prescription.
- Compare two learners' supported handover requests without ranking clinical competence from game scores.

### Manual formative rubric

For each selected case or safety concept, record these three dimensions separately:

| Dimension | Look for |
|---|---|
| Clinical interpretation | Connects supplied findings/trends; distinguishes suspicion from confirmation |
| Priorities and supervised action | Keeps assessment/treatment parallel; identifies senior/team role |
| Uncertainty/request | Communicates limits and a clear urgent/appropriate request |

States: **not observed**, **needs further discussion**, **explains with prompting**, **explains without prompting**. Choose advice based on discussion; free text is not automatically graded. Add an observation, feedback and next step. Reviewer/time/attempt/version are retained; staff-only notes do not alter rewards. Live saving requires configured service access; local demo review is not institutional submission.

## Before student delivery

Approve clinical wording and image rights; pilot on real devices; configure Auth, approved content, course/cohort memberships, faculty assignments, privacy/retention, deadline/class details and support. Apply the migration and deploy functions in reviewed staging first. Neither a public project ID nor a shared course code is authentication.
