# Pilot script and observation form

## Participants

Recruit 4–6 representative clinical-year students with a mix of phone and desktop access. Obtain required consent and accessibility accommodations. Do not use real patient information.

## Tasks

1. Enter the demo and explain what the app claims and does not claim.
2. Download the offline pack, open orientation and one resource.
3. Complete each mission, including one intentional wrong safety choice and correction.
4. Pause, refresh, close and reopen; verify the correct resume point.
5. Finish all 15 decisions, review feedback, correct one intentional miss and submit the team debrief.
6. Go offline, revisit content, reconnect and sync.
7. Find progress, follow-up and help; report one confusing item.
