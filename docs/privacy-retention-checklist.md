# Privacy and retention setup checklist

- [ ] Institution approves the learner identifier and identity provider.
- [ ] Course owner records the lawful/approved purpose and privacy notice.
- [ ] Institution chooses retention and deletion periods; none are invented here.
- [ ] Cohort membership and faculty assignment are verified with test accounts.
- [ ] Editor access is confirmed not to grant learner-result access.
- [ ] No patient identifiers, patient uploads, advertising trackers or public rankings are enabled.
- [ ] Device sign-out warns about unsynced work and prevents cross-account upload.
- [ ] Export recipients and secure storage are approved.
- [ ] Backup, restore, deletion and incident-response owners are named.
- [ ] Offline-device revocation limitations are included in the learner notice.
