# Student guide

For new 15-decision attempts, use the [focused edition instructions](focused-edition-2026-10-08.md). Older saved attempts keep their original questions. The new edition was educator-approved and published on 2026-10-08; live access checks are documented in the release report.

## New learning journey

Keep the team role: read the supplied update and choose an action. At the three handovers, write one short reason connecting findings, priorities and uncertainty **before** revealing choices. References and the patient chart stay available. Confidence is optional and never affects rewards.

After a correction, read its explanation and press the review/collect button. Your original answer stays recorded. Finish the shift after all 15 decisions and your three handovers. Bring the personal learning summary and uncertainties to class; rewards do not establish clinical competence.

Answers remain on this device if offline. New work submits when online, on reconnection and after sign-in recovery. “Completed on this device” is not a course receipt. Check confirmed submission before class; pending or rejected work remains locally recoverable. Demo sync never contacts an institutional service.

## Before class

Allow about 39 minutes: quest briefing 3 minutes, reference cards 9, three patient quests 24, and team debrief 3. These are estimates, not timers.

1. Choose one of four unnamed learner characters. This changes the game appearance, not the scoring or feedback. Use the **ไทย / EN** button at the top to change language; familiar English clinical terms are retained in Thai where they are clearer. Then sign in through the course invitation or institutional route. A course code may select a cohort but is never your identity.
2. Open the offline panel and choose **Download** while connected.
3. Read the team briefing and collect the reference cards.
4. Move with the on-screen direction pad, arrows or WASD. Approach a patient bed; **Enter case** activates nearby. Review the short team update and choose an action. Confidence and notes are optional except for one brief reason or uncertainty at each of the three handovers. The collapsible patient chart keeps earlier evidence and reference cards available.
5. Review the concise authored feedback; expand other explanations if useful. Collect reward after feedback. Correct missed actions in the same numbered decision. Learner reactions celebrate learning or invite reconsideration; patients stay supported and monitored without answer-driven clinical change.
6. Complete the 6+5+4 decisions and six safety badges. Review your three saved handover notes and select **Finish shift** (no additional question). First-correct decisions earn 10, corrected decisions 8 total, each case 15, each safety badge 5 and shift completion 25. Maximum reward is 250; completing everything through corrections earns 220. Both can unlock outfit 5. Outfit thresholds: 0,40,90,150,210.
7. Check the receipt state: **Completed on this device**, **Waiting to sync**, or **Confirmed by course**. At the end, the reward podium compares only total reward with pseudonymous cohort peers. The local demo uses clearly fictional classmates.

Reference cards include selected supplied figures with enlargement, accessible descriptions and source-page links. These are teaching examples, not images of the fictional patients. The case's textual imaging report is authoritative. Local policy and senior review remain essential.

Equal total rewards share rank; speed does not break ties. The podium compares only matching content/reward versions. Replaying or reopening resources never adds reward. A saved original attempt stays on its original questions/scoring; **Open revised cases and rewards** starts a separate revision without deleting it.

Offline files are marked verified only when present in an active service worker's cache. After the first online load of an installed/production build, wait for caching, reload and retry verification if needed. The development preview cannot certify a production offline pack. Keep the same browser/device to resume.

Hold a direction key or touch button to walk; release it to stop. Keyboard activation of a direction button also moves one step. Source details and feedback references can be expanded when needed.

You may pause, close and resume on the same device. Pausing and lower confidence do not reduce points. A wrong answer opens learning; it does not harm a simulated patient or erase progress. Installation is optional. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted, so sync when connected.

This activity reviews reasoning for a supervised role. It is a formative case journey, not a pre-class examination. It does not certify binder application, independent trauma management or procedural competence. Use your institution's approved local pathway in real care.
# Email-and-ID entry, account and movement update

Enter your email address and Student ID, then choose **Enter game**. Keep leading zeros in your ID. Use exactly the same details on another device to resume accepted work. Your identity is self-reported: anyone knowing both details can reopen it. First entry and cross-device retrieval need a connection; unsent work stays on the device where it was created.

Your Student ID appears at the top. Open **Account** for My progress, Sync now, Reset progress and Sign out. **Reset progress** starts a new practice attempt with zero rewards; assessed answers and unsent work are retained. Practice does not replace your assessed result or appear on the official podium.

Drag the round joystick to walk. A longer drag moves faster. Release to stop. Arrow keys/WASD remain available. Approach a patient and choose Enter case. Conflicting submissions from another device preserve accepted answers and appear for review; do not erase local storage to resolve them.
